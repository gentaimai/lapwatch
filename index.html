LapWatch Pro

Stopwatch app for iPhone with hardware volume-button control, lap history, and CSV export.

English

LapWatch Pro is a stopwatch app designed for quick lap timing on iPhone. Use the iPhone hardware volume buttons to start, stop, and record laps. Saved sessions can be reviewed later and exported as CSV.
